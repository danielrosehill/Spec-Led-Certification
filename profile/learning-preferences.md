# Learning preferences

> Filled by the `learning-preferences` skill. The most durable file here — how you
> learn changes far more slowly than what you know. Two-year review interval.

This exists so the recommendation can rank two credentials that score alike on
content by how well their **preparation route** suits you. An exam whose only serious
prep is a 40-hour video course is a different proposition depending on the answers
below, and that difference belongs in the ranking rather than in a footnote.
