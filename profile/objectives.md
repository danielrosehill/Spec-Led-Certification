## Standing positions

Principles the user holds before seeing any options. **Recorded before research
begins, so the research cannot quietly talk them out of it.** Each one becomes either
a scorecard criterion or a hard filter.

| Position | Strength | Becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *No vendor-specific credential without a specific reason* | *strong preference* | *criterion, high weight* |
| *Must be sittable remotely* | *hard requirement* | *filter* |

**Strength** is one of:

- **hard requirement** — a candidate failing this is excluded, not scored down
- **strong preference** — heavy weight on the scorecard
- **preference** — ordinary weight
- **tiebreak** — only matters between otherwise equal candidates

Distinguishing these matters. A hard requirement quietly demoted to a preference is
how a spec-led process turns back into a market-led one.
